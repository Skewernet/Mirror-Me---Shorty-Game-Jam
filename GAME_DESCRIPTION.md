# MIRROR ME

**A first-person psychological horror game for Shorty Game Jam 2026. Theme: INVERTED.**

Mara Vale returns to her mother's house to collect what remains. The fuse has gone again. The telephone carries a familiar voice. In the hall, her reflection is learning the evening's routine.

Explore a cramped, furnished home across seven chapters. Solve each part of the evening before the back door opens. Stepping through it carries you straight into the front hall again, without a fade or a break in your movement. Each return changes what the house remembers.

Read the diary, listen between radio stations and return the keepsakes her hands once knew. Then undo the routine in reverse. The sitting-room telephone carries calls; the separate kitchen radio carries the weather and its own part of the ritual. When the familiar exit is gone, the empty glass offers another way through.

The woman in the mirror no longer moves like you. Bodies fold into impossible postures, crawl through doorways and vanish from rooms you thought were empty. Blood marks familiar care objects. Irregular electrical dips and sounds behind the plaster build toward a house that remembers the night differently. Crossing the glass means facing the part Mara has left out of her account.

MIRROR ME combines a detailed female character, physically based house surfaces, local sound and synthetic dialogue with authored horror encounters. Its first-play target is **10–15 minutes**; that target still needs confirmation with first-time human players.

Built for a desktop browser with keyboard and mouse. Headphones recommended. WASD or arrows to walk, Shift to run, mouse to look, E to interact, F for the flashlight, Tab for inventory/journal, H for a contextual thought and Escape to pause. Settings include brightness, picture quality, walking motion, sound, subtitles, sudden encounters and irregular light flicker.

Contains graphic blood imagery, violent death, family violence and murder, illness, bereavement, crying/sobbing, distorted figures, sudden appearances and flickering lights. The sudden-encounter and light-flicker settings are separate; changing them does not block puzzles or the ending.
