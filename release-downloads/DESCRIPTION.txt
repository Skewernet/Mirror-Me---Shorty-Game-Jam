MIRROR ME
A first-person psychological horror game by Skewer Games.

DON'T LOOK AWAY.

Mara Vale returns to her mother's house. The fuse has gone again. The telephone carries a familiar voice. In the hall, her reflection is learning to move without her.

Explore a cramped home that changes each time you return. Read the diary, follow the evening's rituals, and decide how long you can trust the woman in the glass.

• Seamless looping rooms and a reflection that learns your movements.
• Environmental puzzles, whispered calls and a house full of uneasy details.
• A short, single-player horror story created for Shorty Jam's INVERTED theme.
• Subtitles, brightness, sensitivity and separate sudden-scare/light-flicker settings.

HOW TO PLAY
Desktop browser with keyboard and mouse. Headphones recommended.
WASD / arrows: walk | Shift: run | Mouse: look | E: interact
F: flashlight | Tab: journal | H: optional hint | Esc: pause / release mouse
Click ENTER THE HOUSE to capture the mouse and enable sound. Fullscreen is available from the menu.

CONTENT WARNING
Flashing lights, sudden scares, graphic blood and gore, violence and family violence, illness, bereavement and disturbing imagery.
