The preset labels identify supplied synthetic styles, not actors or cloned
identifiable people. No human performance is claimed.

Tested renderer: sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 CPU, Python 3.12, two inference threads.
English phonemes: lexicon-gb-en.txt and bundled espeak-ng-data, lang='en'.
Sample rate: 24000 Hz. Exact line settings and output/model checksums are in
production.json; source text and voice assignments are in lines.json.

Kokoro v1.0 supersedes the former public-domain LJ Speech/Piper engine in this
revision. No LJ Speech voice is used in the current thirty-nine takes.

Revision 0.7 adds a finite fictional news report and a broken-word Mara take.
The latter uses scripted phrase spacing and restrained amplitude modulation;
it is synthetic TTS, not a recorded human sob or a real-person voice clone.

The local narrative/crime-scene pass adds a photograph memory and three
evidence observations, and re-renders arrival, confession, final accusation
and funeral news with matching source text. Existing supplied presets,
model hashes, normalization and decoded peak checks remain unchanged.

The local visual/parlor pass updates the telephone message and direct 03:17
mirror observation, and adds a finite “Goodnight” at the attack. The new word
uses bf_isabella / speaker22, exactly as Mum on the telephone, at speed1.10
with full-range mastering and no pitch shift.

Second-call performance revision: Mum uses bf_isabella / speaker22, speed0.86,
telephone filtering and a seven-phrase plan with unequal explicit pauses. The
original plea names the music box, bath and radio. production.json records the
measured phrase starts, duration, encoded hash and decoded true peak; these starts
are also the runtime caption cues. No recording or third-party sound was added.

Reflection/routine revision: eight original indirect mirror taunts and one third
Mum call bring the total to39 takes. The third call uses bf_isabella22 and an
eight-phrase plan directing the bedside diary and reverse household sequence.
All twelve THE GLASS takes retain bf_emma21 as their recognizable clear layer,
with an original quieter lowered/raspy layer and deterministic glottal closure
clicks generated by project code. No film/actor recording or external creature
audio is included. tools/generate_voice.py records the full reproducible filter
